When I originally came up with the concept of Jordan Ventures, it was birthed out of a different place and desire that was not as genuine as it feels now. It may have started off *real*, but started to devolve as I felt pressured to perform and use AI to help me "speed up" the process of being seen.

That pressure to perform didn't show up in my last post in the archive, what is now [[The Walkability of Atlanta]]. That post was built out of joy and exploration. No AI. No fluff. Just me doing me. What Jordan Ventures is now is going to continue to follow that trend, but I'll keep that older work accessible to myself and the public just to show my evolution and how I've changed.
