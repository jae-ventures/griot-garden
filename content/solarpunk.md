## What is *solarpunk*?

A radical way to envision a future we *want* to be a part of.

[SolarPunk Cities: Our Last Hope by DamiLee](https://youtu.be/UVlBmdvIC6s?si=lQgTAAFlRr-U9iPZ)

## Why solarpunk?

Because we need hope.

Hope is a catalyst for action.

## My Reading List

My journey with solarpunk began in a series that isn't quite solarpunk, but perhaps was an early precursor to it. The *Earthseed* series by Octacia E. Butler (*Parable of the Sower* and *Parable of the Talents*) paints a bleak near-future America where everything has fallen apart. Radical Christian Nationalists have taken the White House, the nation is in ecological decay, poverty is the baseline, and race is still ever problematic (hmm, sounds familiar). Despite all this despair, the main character somehow learns to carry a hope for a future she believes she can will to life. Her inner monologue and writings of her own religion of sorts are the throughline of hope that carry you through what is otherwise ... a very dark dystopian novel.

After reading Butler's work, I was hooked on the themes of hope and resistance she presented and sought out other works like it. This exploration is what led my to the genre of solarpunk, something that is ever still evolving and is gaining more attention as the people desperately desire to move towards a more socially and ecologically just future.
